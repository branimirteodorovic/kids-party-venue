# 1.5 BookingPolicy

Status: Done
Plan: [1.5 BookingPolicy](../implementation-plan.md#15-bookingpolicy)
Depends on: 1.3 provider entity (done)

## Goal
A provider's booking terms can be stored: whether a deposit is taken and how much, how soon it must be paid, and until when a parent can cancel for free. 1.9 lets providers edit them, 1.16 reuses the type for per-offering overrides, and 5.2 and 5.3 compute and snapshot the terms of an inquiry from it.

## Scope
- In: the `BookingPolicy` value object, the `DepositType` enum and `BookingPolicyErrors` in `Domain/BookingPolicies/`; `Provider.BookingPolicy` (null until set) and `Provider.ChangeBookingPolicy`; the owned-type mapping on `providers`, two check constraints, and the migration.
- Out: the command and endpoint that set the policy (1.9); requiring a policy before review (1.13); showing it on the public page (1.11); per-offering overrides (1.16); the deposit amount, due date and deadlines of a booking (5.2 calculators); the policy snapshot on an inquiry (5.3); refunds and forfeiting on cancellation (5.7); reminders (5.8).

## Acceptance criteria
1. `BookingPolicy.Create(depositType, depositValue, depositDueDays, freeCancellationDays)` returns a policy with those values when they are valid.
2. `DepositType` is `None`, `Percentage` or `FixedAmount`; an undefined value fails with `BookingPolicies.UnknownDepositType`.
3. With `None`, a deposit value or due days given fails with `BookingPolicies.DepositTermsWithoutDeposit`; both stay null.
4. With `Percentage`, the value must be a whole number from 1 to 100, else `BookingPolicies.InvalidDepositPercentage`. With `FixedAmount`, a whole number of dinars from 1 to 100,000, else `BookingPolicies.InvalidDepositAmount`. A missing value fails with the same error.
5. With a deposit, the due days must be 1 to 30 (days after the provider accepts), else `BookingPolicies.InvalidDepositDueDays`.
6. Free cancellation days must be 0 to 180 (days before the party), else `BookingPolicies.InvalidFreeCancellationDays`. 0 means "until the day of the party".
7. Two policies with the same values are equal (record equality).
8. `Provider.Create` leaves `BookingPolicy` null. `Provider.ChangeBookingPolicy(policy, utcNow)` sets it and `UpdatedAtUtc`.
9. A provider saved with a policy is read back with an equal policy; one saved without a policy is read back with null.
10. The database rejects a row whose deposit columns don't match its type (a value or due days with `None`, or a missing one with a deposit), and a row with some policy columns set and `deposit_type` null.

## API
None.

## Data model
Nullable columns on `providers`, mapped as an owned type (as `LocalizedText` is on `cities`):
- `booking_deposit_type varchar(20)` (the enum name), `booking_deposit_value numeric(12,2)`, `booking_deposit_due_days integer`, `booking_free_cancellation_days integer`.
- Check `ck_providers_booking_policy_complete`: either all four are null, or type and free cancellation days are set.
- Check `ck_providers_booking_deposit`: `None` has value and due days null; `Percentage` and `FixedAmount` have both set.
- Ranges stay in Domain only, so a later change to a limit needs no migration.

## Edge cases
- A due date that falls after the party (a party in 2 days, 7 due days) isn't the policy's problem: 5.2 caps the due date at the party.
- Free cancellation days may be 0.
- `FixedAmount` larger than the party's price is accepted here; 5.2 caps the deposit at the quoted price.
- No deposit with free cancellation days set is valid: the provider still asks parents to cancel by then, there's just nothing to forfeit.

## Tests
- Unit (`BookingPolicyTests`): a valid policy of each deposit type; each error in criteria 2–6 with boundary values (0, 1, 100, 101 percent; 0, 1, 100,000, 100,001 dinars; 12.5 percent and 100.50 dinars rejected; 0, 1, 30, 31 due days; −1, 0, 180, 181 cancellation days); a missing value or due days with a deposit; equality. `ProviderTests`: null after create; `ChangeBookingPolicy` sets the policy and `UpdatedAtUtc`, and throws on null.
- Integration (`ProviderPersistenceTests`): the round trip with each deposit type and with no policy; setting and replacing the policy on a provider loaded tracked in a new scope (the 1.9 flow); both check constraints, by raw SQL updates of a saved row (a check violation with the constraint name).

## Decisions
- **The policy is one value object with a `DepositType`**, not a class per type, so 1.9's request and 5.3's snapshot have one flat shape.
- **The days are whole days, counted in `Europe/Belgrade` local dates** by 5.2; the policy only stores the numbers.
- **The percentage is whole and the amount is whole dinars**: nobody quotes a 12.5% deposit or paras. The column is `numeric(12,2)` like later money columns, so the rule can relax without a migration.
- **Columns on `providers`, not a table of their own**: one policy per provider, always loaded with it. 1.16 maps the same type onto offerings.
- **No quantity change deadline** (owner's decision): most venues charge for time, not per guest, and a parent shouldn't pay for children who didn't come. The parent and the venue agree on the final price at the party, and the provider records it (5.6). The plan's "quantity change days" is dropped from 1.5 and 5.2.
- **The policy is null until the provider sets it** (owner's answer): an admin-created, unclaimed profile has no terms yet, and a default would show parents terms the provider never chose. 1.13 requires a policy before review.
- **Deposit due days count from the provider's acceptance** (owner's answer): 5.5 holds the slot at acceptance and 5.7 expires unpaid holds; counting back from the party would let a hold sit for months. 5.2 caps the date at the party.
- **The limits are deposit due 1–30 days, free cancellation 0–180 days and a fixed deposit up to 100,000 RSD** (owner's answer). They only reject typos and can change without a migration.
- **The deposit is paid to the provider directly, not through the platform** (owner's answer): cash, bank transfer or card at the venue, and the provider records it (5.6). The policy says how much and by when, not how.
- **Plan changes in this step** (owner's answers): quantity change days are removed from 1.5 and 5.2, and so is P2.3 (quantity change requests). 5.6 now says that parents pay the provider directly and that the final price is agreed at the party, with an optional IPS QR for the deposit.
- **A domain method `ChangeBookingPolicy` comes now**, so the round trip can be tested; 1.9 only adds the slice.

- **Found during the build:** the migration is `AddProviderBookingPolicy`. Replacing the owned policy on a tracked provider works with the plain mapping, and a test now covers it. The check-constraint tests write the columns with parameterized `ExecuteSqlAsync`, so no SQL is built from strings.
