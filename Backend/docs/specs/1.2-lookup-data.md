# 1.2 Lookup data

Status: Done
Plan: [1.2 Lookup data](../implementation-plan.md#12-lookup-data)
Depends on: 0.9 languages (done), 1.1 cache tags (done)

## Goal
The places and features that providers and parents choose from exist in every environment: all cities and municipalities of Serbia (hidden), the Novi Sad districts and the amenities. Parents' pages get the open cities with their categories and districts, and the registration and profile forms get every city, with names in the request language.

## Scope
- In: the `City`, `District` and `Amenity` entities, the `ProviderCategory` enum, open categories per city (`CityCategory`), the `GeoCoordinate` value object and its mapping to `geography(Point, 4326)`, one migration with the seed data, opening venues in Novi Sad in the development seeder, `GET catalogs` and `GET cities` (cached per language).
- Out: providers and their city, district and amenity references (1.3, 1.7); admin management of districts and amenities (7.6); opening and closing categories through the API, and filling locative names (7.7); search by district or amenity (3.5); district and city landing pages and the sitemap (P2.8); amenities per category (P2.11); `Cache-Control` and `Vary` headers (8.0).

## Acceptance criteria
1. The migration creates the tables below and seeds, in every environment: one city per local self-government unit (grad or opština) in the official register, the Novi Sad districts and the amenities. No category is open in any city.
2. Every seeded city has a unique slug, a Serbian name and a center point inside Serbia's bounding box. Every seeded district and amenity has a slug unique within its city (districts) or overall (amenities) and a Serbian name.
3. In Development, the seeder opens `Venue` in Novi Sad. Running it again changes nothing.
4. `GET catalogs` (anonymous) returns only the cities with at least one open category, each with its slug, name, locative name, center, open categories and districts, plus all amenities.
5. `GET cities` (anonymous) returns every city, open or not, with its slug, name and districts.
6. Names and locative names come back in the request language; a missing English or Russian name falls back to Serbian. No `Accept-Language`, or an unsupported one, means Serbian.
7. Cities and districts are sorted by the returned name with the request language's collation (in Serbian, "Crna Trava" comes before "Čačak"); amenities by their sort order.
8. Both responses are cached per language under the tag `lookups`. A change in the database shows up after `RemoveByTagAsync("lookups")`, not before.
9. `City.OpenCategory` fails with `Cities.LocativeNameRequired` unless the locative name is set in all three languages, and with `Cities.CategoryAlreadyOpen` (conflict) when it is already open. `City.CloseCategory` fails with `Cities.CategoryNotOpen` when it isn't open.
10. `GeoCoordinate.Create` fails with `Geography.InvalidCoordinate` for a latitude outside ±90 or a longitude outside ±180.

## API
- `GET catalogs`: anonymous. `200` with `{ cities: [{ id, slug, name, locativeName, center: { latitude, longitude }, categories: ["Venue"], districts: [{ id, slug, name }] }], amenities: [{ id, slug, name }] }`. No errors.
- `GET cities`: anonymous. `200` with `[{ id, slug, name, districts: [{ id, slug, name }] }]`. No errors.

## Data model
- `cities`: `id uuid` PK; `slug varchar(100)` unique; `name_sr varchar(100) not null`, `name_en`, `name_ru`; `locative_name_sr`, `locative_name_en`, `locative_name_ru` `varchar(100)` null; `center geography(Point, 4326) not null` with a GiST index.
- `districts`: `id uuid` PK; `city_id` FK to `cities` (restrict); `slug varchar(100)`; `name_sr not null`, `name_en`, `name_ru`; unique `(city_id, slug)`.
- `amenities`: `id uuid` PK; `slug varchar(100)` unique; `name_sr not null`, `name_en`, `name_ru`; `sort_order int not null`.
- `city_categories`: `city_id` FK to `cities` (cascade); `category varchar(20)` (`Venue`, `Catering`, `Cakes`); `opened_at_utc timestamptz not null`; PK `(city_id, category)`.

## Edge cases
- A city with districts but no open category is in `GET cities` with its districts, not in `GET catalogs`.
- An open city without districts has `districts: []`.
- A migration that changes seed data doesn't clear the cache of a running API; the expiration bounds it, and a deployment restarts the API anyway.

## Tests
- Unit: `GeoCoordinate` (range checks); `City.Create`, `OpenCategory` (locative required, already open, sets the time), `CloseCategory` (not open); `District.Create` and `Amenity.Create` (Serbian name and slug required).
- Integration:
  - the seed: every city has a center inside Serbia, Novi Sad and Belgrade exist, slugs are unique, the Novi Sad districts and amenities exist
  - the development seeder opened `Venue` in Novi Sad, and running it again keeps one row
  - `GET catalogs` lists Novi Sad with `Venue` and its districts, and leaves out a seeded city with nothing open
  - `GET cities` lists every seeded city
  - names with `Accept-Language` `en`, `ru` and `de`, and the Serbian fallback for a missing Russian name
  - the Serbian sort order ("Crna Trava" before "Čačak")
  - the cache, for both endpoints: a name changed with SQL stays old until `RemoveByTagAsync("lookups")`
  - both endpoints answer without a token

## Decisions
- **A city is a local self-government unit.** Belgrade, Novi Sad and Niš are one row each; their city municipalities and suburbs (Zemun, Petrovaradin, Niška Banja) become districts when the city opens.
- **A city is open when at least one category is open in it.** No separate visibility flag, so the two can't disagree. Closing deletes the row; the audit log (1.14) keeps the history.
- **Seed data goes in `HasData`**, as the migration skill says, read from CSV files embedded in Infrastructure (`Database/Seed`), so the data can be reviewed apart from the migration. IDs are fixed GUIDs in the files. The city list follows the Law on Territorial Organization, and the centers are each seat's coordinates in Wikidata (CC0). Each file notes its source.
- **English names are stored only where they differ** (Belgrade); the rest fall back to Serbian.
- **`ProviderCategory` has `Venue`, `Catering` and `Cakes`** now, as the plan's wording for 1.8 and 1.12 assumes. It's stored and serialized as its name (the global string enum converter).
- **`LocalizedText` is mapped as an owned type** in its owner's table, one column per language as decided in 0.9. Found during the build: `HasData` silently leaves out complex-type columns, so the planned complex type couldn't be seeded.
- **`GeoCoordinate` lives in Domain** and is mapped to `Point` with a value converter, as the Key decisions table says; 1.3 reuses it.
- **No repositories yet.** Nothing writes lookup data through a command until 7.6 and 7.7; the development seeder uses the `DbContext`, like the admin profile.
- **`GET cities` is anonymous.** It's public data, and anonymity makes it cacheable and usable before sign-up.
- **The cache tag is `lookups`**, without an ID, because every lookup write affects both responses. Expiration: 1 hour.
- **Kosovo and Metohija units are seeded**, hidden like every city, so the list matches the register and a provider there can register.
- **Locative names are nullable** and seeded in all three languages for Novi Sad only; the admin fills them in when opening a city (7.7 already requires them). Seeding a correct locative for every city is error-prone.
- **Russian names are seeded for every city** by transliterating the official Serbian Cyrillic name, and reviewed before a city opens. The plan's 1.2 bullet says so.
- **Novi Sad districts:** Centar, Liman, Grbavica, Adice, Telep, Detelinara, Novo naselje, Satelit, Podbara, Salajka, Rotkvarija, Klisa, Sajlovo, Avijatičarsko naselje, and the places Petrovaradin, Sremska Kamenica, Veternik, Futog, Kać, Budisava, Kovilj, Ledinci, Bukovac, Rumenka, Kisač, Čenej, Stepanovićevo, Begeč.
- **Amenities, in this order:** parking, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, outdoor area, café for parents, stroller access, baby changing table, baby corner, trampoline, play maze, ball pit, sound system, projector. Smoking area, animator and catering rules are venue details (1.6), not amenities.
- **Found during the build:** the law's text online stops partway through the list, so the 145 units outside Kosovo and Metohija come from Wikipedia's tables, which number them as the law does, and the 29 there (28 municipalities and Priština) from the law's own list. Wikidata gives only the seat coordinates.
- **Found during the build:** Russian names follow the usual Russian spelling of Serbian places: hyphenated (Нови-Сад, Петровац-на-Млави), with ј, љ, њ, ћ, ђ and џ written as Russian letters (Крагуевац, Валево, Чуприя, Аранджеловац). Belgrade is Белград.
- **Found during the build:** slugs are written from the Serbian name, amenities included (`klima-uredjaj`), because the frontend's URLs and query values are Serbian. The district Centar is "City Centre" in English and "Центр" in Russian.
- **Found during the build:** `locativeName` falls back to the city's name when no locative is stored; an open city always has one, so this only guards the response shape.
- **Found during the build:** sorting uses Postgres's ICU collations (`sr-Latn-x-icu`, `en-x-icu`, `ru-x-icu`), chosen per language inside the SQL, so the language stays a parameter. They come with the `postgis/postgis` image.
- **Found during the build:** the slices live in one `Lookups` area (`Application/Lookups/GetCatalogs`, `GetCities`, `Presentation/Lookups`), with the tag and expiration in `LookupsCache`. Slug rules are in Domain's `LookupSlug`, and `City.OpenCategory` also rejects an undefined category (`Cities.UnknownCategory`).
- **Found during the build:** the register has 174 units, so the seed and `GET cities` tests expect 174 cities.
